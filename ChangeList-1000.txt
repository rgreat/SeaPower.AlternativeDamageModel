Version 1.7.0.

Changes:
• Auto DC: the first team in a burning section fights the fire; counterflooding goes first at a critical heel; a lost engine is marked for priority repair (then only the engine and its fires are worked on).
• Order: ... > Counterflooding > Flooding > Flooded Structure > Systems > Structure. No pumping that worsens the list or trim.
• Critical state: only the wounded rest; fresh teams relieve tired ones; the Status card (was Forecast) shows a red Critical. Free teams finish small repairs.
• New: System Repair / Hull Repair speed, Fire growth limit.
• Sinking by the deck: both central points or 6 of them under the sea for 10 s. List and trim are kept after a load.
• Fixed a game bug: holes grew while a team worked in the neighbouring section.
• Tooltips: system repair speed, time to the next level, who repairs it; they update while open.
• Faster at high time compression.
