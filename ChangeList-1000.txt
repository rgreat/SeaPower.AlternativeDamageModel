Version 1.8.0.

Changes:
- Supports Sea Power 0.9.0 only.
- Settings window: "profile: x" next to "game: x"; the reset button brings back the profile's value.
- New tab Collisions: ships slow down and lose hull where they touch (the bow takes less than the side); strong rams open leaks; the water holds the struck ship. Side-by-side touches hardly slow the ships.
- Settings: damage, flooding, minimum speed, bow strength, slowing, water resistance, pressing, AI ships, push apart.
- Auto DC does not repair an engine put out by flooding until the water is out.
- Small ships (1000 t down to 50 t) burn and flood up to 3x faster.
- Hit list shows secondary explosions of systems.
- Systems go out of order from 75% of water in a compartment or 50% in the section.
