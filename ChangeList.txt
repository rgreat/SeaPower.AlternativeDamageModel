Alternative Damage Model - change list

Version 1.0.0 (build 20261015, for Sea Power 0.8.4)

Damage Control interface
- Damage control teams as people: number by displacement, walking between sections, muster time, knock-outs (a team knocked out twice is lost).
- Smarter Auto DC while transfers take time.
- DC Priority for the whole ship: Auto, Fire first, Flooding first.
- NEW: "Limit Speed" button next to Auto DC (on by default). Off removes the game's cut of the ship's speed by flooding.
- Orders to single teams with Auto DC off: task, move, send to the reserve; counterflooding.
- Detailed Damage Control tab: hull integrity of each side, one icon per team, water and fire in % with trend arrows.
- Tooltips of water and fire explain why: what raises the value and what lowers it, who is plugging or pumping, speed effects.
- LIST / TRIM / LEVEL gauges; pointing at them shows the flooding plan (above the card).
- NEW: "Show deck levels in 3D" check box in the flooding plan: the ship's deck points are shown in the 3D view all the time.

Damage model (Advanced profile)
- Stronger hulls (factors per ship type and per ship), tunable side effects of hits, stronger systems.
- No instant sinking from list or trim: water over the deck edge, centre of mass rising with the water, capsizing, wreck drag, bubbles stopped after 10 minutes.
- Fire and flooding speeds; fires that nobody fights grow faster.
- NEW: the ship's speed changes how fast water comes in (through holes and over the deck edge) and how fast fires grow: a table of speed points (Settings -> Damage Model -> Ship speed). Advanced: 0..50 knots, water x0.75..x5, fire x1..x2.
- NEW: teams cannot plug holes above a set speed (DC Teams -> "Max ship speed for plugging holes"; Advanced 15 knots, 0 = no limit).
- NEW: a team plugging holes also pumps out water (setting "Pumping while plugging holes").
- NEW: pumping out starts from the side of a section with more water and changes sides at most every 10 seconds, or when this side is at its minimum.
- NEW: fire and smoke points by ship length: under 25 m one, under 50 m two, under 75 m three, from 75 m five.
- Team effectiveness 0.3 in the Advanced profile.

Settings
- Settings window (the gear next to (i)): profiles Base Game, Advanced and your own; changes apply at once. The reset button restores the game's value.
- One switch turns the whole damage model off and on during a mission.
