Version 1.7.0.

- Auto DC: the first team in a burning section fights the fire (other work there gets at least two teams); counterflooding gets one team and goes first when list or trim are critical; a lost engine is marked for priority repair automatically, and until it works again the teams only fight the fires at the engine and repair it.
- New order: Flooding Rate > Fire Rate > Fires > Counterflooding > Flooding > Flooded Structure > Systems > Structure. Flooded Structure is hull repair where the damaged hull keeps the water in. The DC Priority hints and the Auto Damage Control Status line show what is first right now.
- Counterflooding starts in the red zone of the list / trim and floods only the side that levels the ship (judged by the deck edges nearest the sea). Auto DC teams do not pump where that would worsen the list or trim.
- Rest: on a ship in a critical state (sinking; water 20%+ with a hole open; hull under 50% with fire or leaks; heavy fires) only the wounded rest. Fresh teams from the reserve relieve tired ones; the last team at a fire or in a section with a badly damaged hull stays. The Forecast card is now the Status card; it shows a red Critical (a yellow Uncontrolled Fires while only fires no team fights yet burn).
- Free teams finish the small repairs when nothing else is left. Auto DC reacts to new damage at once.
- New settings: System Repair and Hull Repair speed (Team speed), Fire growth limit (Fire and flooding).
- A ship with 5 sections sinks when both central deck points of its middle section, or at least 6 deck points, stay under the sea for 10 seconds. A section puts its fire out under water only when both of its deck points are under the sea.
- The list and trim are saved with the game and restored after a load.
- Fixed a game bug: holes of a section grew while a team worked in the neighbouring one.
- System tooltips: repair speed, time to the next level and the teams repairing it; team tooltips: efficiency for the work the team does now. Tooltips update while open.
- Map icons of your own ship do not show hull damage that cannot be welded any more or water that cannot be pumped out.
- Much less load at high time compression (the heel checks are no longer recomputed for every team every physics step).
