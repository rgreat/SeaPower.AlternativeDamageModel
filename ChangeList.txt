Version 1.8.0.

- Supports Sea Power 0.9.0 only; the code for older game versions is removed.
- Settings window: every profile setting shows "profile: x" (the value stored in the profile) under "game: x"; the row's reset button (↺) brings back the profile's value (the mod's global options still go back to their default). Tab Collisions is the last one before Debug; Debug is always the last tab.
- Debug trace: a line for every hit (section, damage, integrity and leak of the compartment afterwards); the log says when another mod takes over Blastzone.OnHitUnit.
- New: collisions between ships (own "Collisions" tab). The game only lets ships pass through each other; now a blow slows them (across the contact almost all the closing speed is lost, the lighter ship the most; along it only a part, so ships touching side by side hardly slow), the water holds the struck ship back and a strong ram shoves it aside. Each ship loses hull where the hulls touch, for as long as they stay in contact: the damage is the energy the blow wears down, the bow takes several times less than the side. Strong blows open leaks (a multiplier for the flooding is set).
- Settings: Enable collision damage, Enable collisions for AI ships, Collision damage, Flooding from collisions, Minimum collision speed, Bow strength, Slowing down, Water resistance, Pressing speed loss, Push ships apart.
- Built-in profiles have a [Collisions] section (Base Game: off). A profile of yours without [Profile] Parent is read as made from Advanced; Apply on the new tab works.
- Auto DC: an engine put out by the flooding of a section is not repaired while that section is flooded (no priority, no team sent to it); the repair goes on when the water is pumped out.
- Small ships burn and flood faster: the growth of fires and the water coming in rise smoothly from 1x at 1000 tons to 3x at 50 tons (setting "Small ships: faster", 1 = off).
- Hit list: secondary explosions of the ship's own systems are named ("Explosion of <system>") and recorded with their section and side.
- Systems of a section go out when one of its compartments holds 75% of water or more, or the section as a whole 50% or more (was: the mean above 75%).
