Alternative Damage Model - change list

Changes since the Workshop version (build 20261011.0026), for Sea Power 0.8.4.
Settings marked [Advanced] are on in the Advanced profile and off (or as in the game) in Base Game.

DAMAGE CONTROL TAB
- Hull Damage and Flooding at the top right also show the overall % of the ship; the hull damage words change at 25 / 50 / 75%.
- Hull integrity arrows are yellow / orange / red while the hull can still be welded, grey when it cannot be welded any further.
- Weapon buttons of the bottom panel are coloured by weapon state: part of a type inoperable - yellow, all of it - red.
- Tooltips of water and fire explain the cause: what raises the value and what lowers it, how fast water comes in through holes and over the deck edge, how fast teams pump, the effect of the ship's speed. A team on its way is said last. Only what changes the value is shown.
- Tooltips on the % values. Arrows no longer flicker: smoothed trend with hysteresis; % values keep their place next to the text.
- The team row of a section is lower and tighter; the ship's picture no longer changes the height of its row.
- NEW: "Limit Speed" button next to Auto DC (on by default). Off removes the game's cut of the ship's speed by flooding.
- NEW: new team task by hand - counterflooding (lets water into the side opposite to the heel; limit settable). Team menu entry "Auto" instead of "By priority".
- Flooding plan (pointing at LIST / TRIM / LEVEL): shown above the gauges; shows the tilt left before a deck edge touches the sea; the limits caption is in two lines.
- NEW: "Show deck levels in 3D" check box in the flooding plan: the ship's deck points are shown in the 3D view all the time.

DAMAGE MODEL
- Water over the deck edge: for ships with a complete hull collider each deck-edge point that stays under the sea (3 s, settable) floods its compartment; a full compartment passes the water on to its neighbours. It replaces the fast flooding from the LIST / TRIM red zones for these ships.
- The red zones and scales of the LIST and TRIM gauges start where the first deck-edge point touches the flat sea, separately for port, starboard, stern and bow.
- A ship sinks from flooding only when both central compartments (or at least 5 of 10) are under the sea.
- Capsizing: the more water in the ship, the higher its centre of mass (setting "Centre of mass rise"): a flooded ship turns keel up. [Advanced]
- Sinking: the centre of mass is not thrown to the hull origin when the ship starts to sink; a wreck loses its way sooner (setting "Wreck drag", horizontal only; 3x); the bubbles effect of a sunk ship stops after 10 minutes. [Advanced]
- A destroyed ship's water level is followed by the mod (the game stops sampling the waves for it).
- NEW: the ship's speed changes how fast water comes in and fires grow: a table of speed points (Settings -> Damage Model -> Ship speed). [Advanced: 0..50 knots, water x0.75..x5, fire x1..x2]
- NEW: teams cannot plug holes above a set speed (DC Teams -> "Max ship speed for plugging holes"; [Advanced] 15 knots).
- NEW: a team plugging holes also pumps out water (setting "Pumping while plugging holes"). [Advanced]
- NEW: pumping starts from the side with more water and changes sides at most every 10 seconds, or when this side is at its minimum.
- A fire does not spread into a section where a team is (setting "Team stops fire spread"); a section under the sea puts its fire out (setting "Fire under water"). [Advanced]
- NEW: fire and smoke points by ship length: under 25 m one, under 50 m two, under 75 m three, from 75 m five.
- Advanced defaults: team effectiveness 0.3, fire damage to the hull 0.1, flood imbalance effect 1.0.

SETTINGS WINDOW
- Drawn close cross; the window can be resized by its edges; wider, with a new "Sinking" tab and groups moved between tabs.
- The reset button of a row restores the game's value (not the profile's).
- Fixed: "Team stops fire spread" and similar switches went back to off when Apply was pressed.

DEBUG (Settings -> Debug)
- Deck point heights, hull collider overlay and ship model view (Show / Translucent / Hide) for the selected ship.
