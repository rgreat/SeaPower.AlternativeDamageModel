Version 1.6.0.

Changes since version 1.4.0 (the last Steam release), for Sea Power 0.8.6-0.8.7.
[Advanced] = on in the Advanced profile only.

- New: settings also open from the main menu and the pause menu. Settings are kept in user/AlternativeDamageControl now (the old folder is moved).
- The Forecast card is shown only while the ship has damage; the health bar of a wounded team is drawn above its icon, not over it. A team with a task given by hand is marked by a small red ! (also on its icon in the orders menu) instead of the yellow underline.
- Fixed: the game crashed at start (a Noesis property of the map was read before the UI was ready); it is read at the first note in a mission now.
- NEW (Systems tab) [Advanced]: "Several repair priorities": the "!" of several systems in a section can be on at once; the section repairs them in turn, in the order they were set.
- Changed: counterflooding floods or pumps out a side only where that takes off the list or trim (the more dangerous first), keeps its action 10 s, and floods nothing in the red band of LEVEL.
- NEW [Advanced] (DC Teams > Water): "Auto DC counterflooding": while holes let water in and list / trim reach 40% of the red zone (until 20%), Auto DC sends a team to counterflood where it helps most. Plugging holes or pumping in a burning section: two teams, one fights the fire.
- Auto, Fire and Flooding on the DC Priority buttons and in the orders menu are now the game's own texts (translated by the game).
- Special effects (extra fire / smoke points, fire looks, sinking bubbles, hiding unseen targets in 3D) moved to Alternative Special Effects; install it to keep them. Settings are taken over on its first start.
