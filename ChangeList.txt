Alternative Damage Model - change list

Changes since the Workshop version (build 20261011.0026), for Sea Power 0.8.3-0.8.5.
[Advanced] = on in the Advanced profile only.

DAMAGE CONTROL TAB
- Hull Damage and Flooding also show the overall % of the ship; weapon buttons are coloured by weapon state.
- Tooltips of water and fire show what raises and what lowers the value (holes, deck-edge water, pumping, ship speed).
- Arrows no longer flicker; the team row of a section is tighter.
- NEW: "Limit Speed" button next to Auto DC: off removes the game's cut of the ship's speed by flooding.
- NEW: counterflooding as a team task by hand.
- Flooding plan: above the LIST / TRIM / LEVEL gauges, shows the tilt left before a deck edge touches the sea.
- NEW: "Show deck levels in 3D" check box in the flooding plan.

DAMAGE MODEL
- Water over the deck edge floods compartments for ships with a complete hull collider; LIST / TRIM red zones start where a deck edge touches the sea.
- A ship sinks from flooding only when its central compartments (or 5 of 10) are under the sea.
- [Advanced] Flooded ships turn keel up (centre of mass rises with the water); wrecks lose their way sooner; bubbles stop after 10 minutes.
- NEW: ship speed changes how fast water comes in and fires grow (table of speed points, Settings -> Damage Model).
- NEW: teams cannot plug holes above a set speed ([Advanced] 15 knots).
- NEW: plugging teams also pump out water; pumping starts from the side with more water.
- [Advanced] A fire does not spread into a section with a team; a section under the sea puts its fire out.
- NEW: fire and smoke points by ship length: <25 m 1, <50 m 2, <75 m 3, from 75 m 5.
- Advanced defaults: team effectiveness 0.3, fire damage to the hull 0.1, flood imbalance effect 1.0.

SETTINGS WINDOW
- Resizable, wider, new "Sinking" tab; the reset button restores the game's value.
- Fixed: "Team stops fire spread" and similar switches went back to off on Apply.
