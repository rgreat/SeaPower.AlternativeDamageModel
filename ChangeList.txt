Alternative Damage Model - change list

Changes since the Workshop version (build 20261015.0114), for Sea Power 0.8.3-0.8.5.
[Advanced] = on in the Advanced profile only.

- Fixed: arrows of fires and flooding no longer slide onto the % text when a colour or a level changes.
- Fixed: DC teams no longer go back to the reserve (or are taken off a section) while it still has hull or systems work.
- Auto DC takes the fastest growing fire or leak first: Fire Rate > Flooding Rate > Fires > Flooding > Systems > Structure (Fire / Flooding priority change the order of their group).
- NEW: Flooding tab: list of ships that get no Deck Points (flooded by the old rules).
- A surface ship without weapons is counted as civilian (hull value, number of teams).
- NEW: AI ships under 50 m ignore the speed limit from flooding (kamikaze) [Advanced].
- Removed: "Centre of mass rise" (not needed).
- "Keep centre of mass", "Wreck drag" and "Stop bubbles after" moved to the Effects tab and are global (not part of a profile); the Sinking tab is gone.
- NEW (Flooding tab) [Advanced]: fire in a section reopens plugged leaks (0.5% of the largest leak a second at fire 100%, up to 50% of it).
- NEW (Flooding tab) [Advanced]: a section flooded above 75% loses its systems (inoperable, no repair) and its fire is put out (10% a second).
- NEW (Fire tab) [Advanced]: teams in a burning section that do not fight the fire work at 75% effectiveness.
- Own profiles (Custom) remember the profile they were made from; settings a profile does not have yet take the values of that parent profile (Base Game if it is gone).
- NEW (DC Teams tab) [Advanced]: team health by ship displacement (hint in %, health bar over the icon, dead at 0%); wounds from hits (a knock-out wounds the team, it walks to the reserve) and from fires it does not fight; recovery in the reserve (0.5% a second); wounded teams work slower.
- NEW (DC Teams tab) [Advanced]: team fatigue (minutes of work, then half speed; the reserve rests it as fast); shown in the hint, tired teams are highlighted; "Wounded" / "Tired" added to the speed lines.
- Removed: Knock-outs settings (Recovery time, Recovery spread, Knock-outs to death).
- DC Teams tab is split into Teams / Work / Health pages; team health has its own on/off switch (off in Base Game, its other settings are hidden when off).
