Version 1.4.0.

Changes since the Workshop version (build 20261015.0114), for Sea Power 0.8.6-0.8.7.
[Advanced] = on in the Advanced profile only.

- New: (i) after "Wound chance" in the settings shows a table of wound chances calculated from the game's own ships and ammunition (mods included). Under each chance it shows the average wound in % of the team's health (follows "Wound strength").
- Changed: water now limits fire in a section at any flooding level: a fire cannot be stronger than 100% minus the section's average flooding % (it dies down to that limit; Full flooding > Fire put out by water, 0 = off).
- Fixed: Wreck drag no longer floods the log with Unity warnings (and lags the game) once a wreck rests on the bottom.
- Fixed: arrows of fires and flooding no longer slide onto the % text when a colour or a level changes.
- Fixed: DC teams no longer go back to the reserve (or are taken off a section) while it still has hull or systems work.
- Auto DC takes the fastest growing leak or fire first: Flooding Rate > Fire Rate > Fires > Flooding > Systems > Structure (Fire / Flooding priority change the order of their group). In a section with a fire (any strength) one team always fights it.
- NEW (Damage Control Teams > Weapon hits): concussion. A team wounded by a hit stays in the reserve for Concussion time (+- spread) after it gets there; A concussed team is out of action: no Auto DC and no order by hand can send it until the concussion has passed. It runs next to the team's health and recovery.
- The Forecast card is centred between its neighbours and shown only while the ship has damage; the health bar of a wounded team is drawn above its icon, not over it. Teams get tired while walking as they do at work. A team with a task given by hand is marked by a small red ! (also on its icon in the orders menu) instead of the yellow underline.
- NEW (User Interface > Map): four small icons under a ship's icon on the map (yours too): fire (total strength of the fires), flooding, hull (classes of the Damage Control panel), engine (yellow / orange / red), and a greyer map icon for a ship that will sink. A contact: all four in direct sight, the engine only by sonar, a '?' guess about a slow ship hit lately by radar, nothing by ESM.
- The Fires line of the Damage Control tab now classes the fires by their total strength (the number of burning sections stays in brackets).
- NEW (Damage Control tab): a "Forecast" card in the empty middle of the summary row: Stable / Sinks in ~N min (by flooding or hull damage) / Sinking, by the trend of the last minute.
- The hit statistics (tooltip of Hull Damage / Flooding / Fires) and the sinking forecast trend are saved with the game and restored on load.
- NEW (User Interface > Map): the Unit Status window of a ship that is not yours (it was empty) shows a Damage State tab with a simplified panel: the four icons in words and a sinking forecast, by what the sensors tell (nothing without direct sight).
- NEW: Flooding tab: list of ships that get no Deck Points (flooded by the old rules).
- A surface ship without weapons is counted as civilian (hull value, number of teams).
- NEW: AI ships under 50 m ignore the speed limit from flooding (kamikaze) [Advanced].
- Removed: "Centre of mass rise" (not needed).
- "Keep centre of mass", "Wreck drag" and "Stop bubbles after" moved to the Effects tab and are global (not part of a profile); the Sinking tab is gone.
- NEW (Flooding tab) [Advanced]: fire in a section reopens plugged leaks (0.5% of the largest leak a second at fire 100%, up to 50% of it).
- NEW (Flooding tab) [Advanced]: a section flooded above 75% loses its systems (inoperable, no repair) and its fire is put out (10% a second).
- NEW (Fire tab) [Advanced]: teams in a burning section that do not fight the fire work at 75% effectiveness.
- Own profiles (Custom) remember the profile they were made from; settings a profile does not have yet take the values of that parent profile (Base Game if it is gone).
- NEW (DC Teams tab) [Advanced]: team health (1-100 by ship displacement; 100 = a crew of 1000+), wounds by the warhead power of the hit (hint in %, health bar over the icon, dead at 0%); wounds from hits (a knock-out wounds the team, it walks to the reserve) and from fires it does not fight; recovery in the reserve (0.5% a second); wounded teams work slower.
- NEW (DC Teams > Health) [Advanced]: a hit wounds a team by chance: a chance that grows steeply with the warhead power against the team health, the weakest hit has a tiny chance, the wound grows up to the warhead power; two multipliers: wound chance (a plain multiplier of the probability; grows steeply with the power and falls with the health, over 100% for a far stronger warhead) and wound strength.
- NEW (DC Teams tab) [Advanced]: team fatigue, two levels (tired after 60, exhausted after 120 minutes), speed 100% > 75% > 25% in straight lines between them (settable; the reserve rests it at a set % of that speed, 100 by default); shown in the hint, tired teams are highlighted; "Wounded" / "Tired" added to the speed lines.
- NEW (DC tab): tooltip of "Hull Damage / Flooding / Fires" lists every hit on the ship (time, ammunition, section, side; over 20 hits: count by ammunition).
- NEW (DC tab): tooltip of a damaged system: damage level, integrity, repair speed, what it waits for.
- NEW (Damage Model tab) [Advanced]: "Damage spills over": damage that a destroyed compartment cannot take goes half to its neighbour, a quarter to the next if that is destroyed too.
- Removed: Knock-outs settings (Recovery time, Recovery spread, Knock-outs to death).
- DC Teams tab is split into Teams / Movement / Work / Health pages; team health has its own on/off switch (off in Base Game, its other settings are hidden when off).
- DC Teams tab: pages Count / Movement / Work / Health / Wounds / Fatigue; team health and team fatigue have their own switches above the pages (off in Base Game; a switched-off page is hidden). Settings columns follow the window size.
- NEW (DC Teams > Health) [Advanced]: "Wounds in reserve" - the teams rest in the central compartment, a hit on it can wound a resting team.
- NEW (DC Teams > Wounds) [Advanced]: "Wounds at any hull integrity" - every hit on a section with teams can wound them, not only under 50% integrity as in the game.
- NEW: Auto DC rests the teams. Auto DC takes a team with health under 33%, or an exhausted one, off its section to the reserve and uses it again only after a full rest (full health, no fatigue). On a ship in a bad way (sinking, or fire, water or hull at the third stage or worse) the rest is shorter: health 50% and fatigue down to fresh (not yet tired). Works with the optimised Auto DC (walking transfers) and the team health / fatigue settings.
- NEW [Advanced] (Systems tab): "DC efficiency without engines" (NoEngineTeamEffectiveness, 50 = % effectiveness; 100 = off). While the ship's Engine (the power system in use, not the propulsion / screws) is out of order, its damage control teams work at that effectiveness at fires, leaks, water and hull (repair of systems is not affected). Shown in the teams' hints ("Engine out: 50% effectiveness") and as a warning in the Forecast line of the Damage Control tab.
- Auto DC no longer leaves free teams idle while a section has work: when nobody else is needed, it adds one more team to the section with the fewest teams every 10 s (up to 4 in a section), from the reserve or from a section with no work.
- Faster: repeated work in the mod's per-tick and per-second code was cut (the ship's speed is read once per frame, one UI tree walk every 2 s instead of two every second, fewer allocations in flooding code).
- Map damage icons now sit under all other map objects. Light hull damage (and hull damage repaired as far as possible) and light flooding of other ships are not shown; an enemy's engine damage is shown only after hits on the ship are registered; the source line lists all sources (visual, sonar, radar).
- NEW (User Interface > Map): "Hide unseen targets in 3D" (default off): contacts not observed visually are not drawn in the 3D view.
- Fixed: list and trim of fast planing boats and hydrofoils no longer include the game's built-in speed and turn tilt (Damage State and the DC gauges).
- Fixed: deck points are no longer refused for some ships whose ini length differs from the model (Komar, Pegasus, Don, Luda I): if the ini length check fails, the hull collider is checked against the model.
- Fixed: deck points are no longer refused for Luda I, Pegasus and Don (their hull colliders are longer than the ini length, which is fine; only a collider that is too short is refused).
- Fixed: no sinking forecast by flooding for a ship whose flooding is not detected.
- Damage State: the engine line now says by which sources it is known (visual, sonar, radar).
- Works with Sea Power 0.8.7 (and still with 0.8.6): the game's new enemy-side rules for hit-wounded damage control teams, and the new map icon refresh, are followed.
- NEW: the mod's texts are translated: English, Russian, Chinese, German, Spanish, French, Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese. The language is the game's; User Interface > Language ("Mod language", shown after the window is reopened) or [General] Language in the .cfg sets another one. The texts are in the lang folder next to the dll (lang/<code>.ini; copy en.ini to make a new language). The translations are machine-made, Korean / Japanese / Chinese / Vietnamese are not checked by native speakers; names that come from the game stay as they are.
- Fixed: the Forecast card was not centred on the Damage Control tab (it sat to the right when the Auto DC card is beside it); it is centred on the ship image now.
- Faster: the per-tick code of flooding, trim, sections and damage control teams reads the game's fields without the slow reflection path (it falls back to the old way by itself if a game update changes a field).
- NEW (DC Teams > Weapon hits) [Advanced]: "Wounds in reserve chance" (HitsInReserveChance, Advanced 0.5): a multiplier of the chance that a hit on the central compartment wounds a team resting in the reserve, on top of Wound chance.
- The Multipliers by speed table (Damage Model) is turned: one point per row, so it fits the form however many points there are.
- The settings window shows "default: x" and under it "game: y" (the value of the Base Game profile where the game has such a rule), or "game: no analogue" for mechanics the game does not have; the reset button says what it resets to.
- Changed: the Advanced profile takes the author's values: Warships 3.0, Damage spills over off, Time under water 5 s, Water over the deck 0.5, faster fire growth (0.3) and hull damage by fire 0.5 (spread chance 2.5%), speed multipliers 2/1.75, 3/3, 5/5 above 30 kn, team effectiveness 0.5, Transfer speed 100, Tired after 30 min, Exhausted after 60 min, Recovery speed 200%, Speed: tired 90%.
